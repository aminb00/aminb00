## Experience

### Research Intern @ Universiteit Antwerpen
**Sep 2025 - Jan 2026 | ADReM Data Lab, Antwerp (BE)**
- Designed an analytical pipeline from raw MS data to sparse deconvolution using MSFragger-DDA+, IonQuant, Prosit deep learning, and LASSO-based spectral decomposition.
- Performed large-scale fragment ion annotation, spectral similarity analysis, and statistical validation of fragmentation competition in chimeric spectra.
- Investigated the relationship between MS1 abundance and MS2 fragmentation dominance.

### Geo-spatial Data Scientist @ University of Bergamo
**Feb 2025 - Nov 2025 | PNRR GRINS, Dept. of Economics**

- Leveraged HPC workflows for large-scale spatio-temporal processing.
